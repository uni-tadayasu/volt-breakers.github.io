# Art Direction — IRON VANGUARD X
- Style: glossy semi-realistic mecha / sci-fi action, 22nd-century reploid war. Cinematic, dramatic.
- Palette: deep navy night, neon cyan & magenta accents, hot orange explosions; each boss has a signature color.
- Lighting: strong rim light, emissive neon trims, volumetric haze.
- Subjects: blue armored buster hero (Van), crimson saber hero with blonde ponytail (Rei), robot-master bosses, bald caped warlord final boss, mechaniloid enemies.
- UI: hand-built industrial plates, rivets, hazard stripes, dot-gothic type (CSS, not images).
- Prompts: original designs only, no franchise names; consistent anime-mecha realism.
