# Sound Direction
- Music: very cool, high-energy rock/synth hybrid (driving drums, distorted guitar riff, arpeggiated synth), loopable 15s; boss fights replay faster.
- SFX (generated): buster shot, big explosion.
- Procedural WebAudio: jump, dash, charge, slash, hurt, item, warning siren, menu blips.
- Audio unlocks on first tap at the title; BGM/SFX volume in Options.
